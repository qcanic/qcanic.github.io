About Me

I am a master’s student in computer architecture at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). I am affiliated with the State Key Laboratory of Cyberspace Security Defense at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences. My research interests include processor security and electronic design automation (EDA), particularly AI-guided hardware design space exploration and hardware architectures for efficient large language model inference.

I am currently a RISC-V security intern with the CVM Group at the Beijing Institute of Open Source Chip. I design and verify branch prediction defenses for XiangShan’s Kunminghu core and use XS-GEM5 to evaluate their impact on processor performance.

I received my B.Eng. in computer science and technology from the College of Information and Electrical Engineering at China Agricultural University in 2025.
