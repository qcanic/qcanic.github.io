About Me

I’m Qiu Can, an M.S. student at the School of Cyber Security, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). I earned my B.Eng. from the College of Information and Electrical Engineering at China Agricultural University.

My work combines processor microarchitecture, performance modeling, and security verification.

Research Interests

  • Electronic design automation (EDA) · Using AI to guide hardware design space exploration and exploring hardware architectures to improve the efficiency of AI workloads such as large language model inference.
  • Processor security · Secure processor microarchitecture, with a focus on branch prediction and speculative execution.
